🏅 School Director

Dr. Rajesh Kumar

Ph.D. in Child Education · Founder & Director
20+

500+

12+
Awards Won

Dr. Rajesh Kumar founded Little Stars Play School in 2009 with a singular vision: to create a space where every child could discover the joy of learning. With a Ph.D. in Child Education from Delhi University and over two decades of experience in early childhood development, Dr. Kumar has been a transformative force in shaping hundreds of young lives.

His philosophy centers on respecting each child's individuality, fostering intrinsic motivation, and creating partnerships between school and home. Under his leadership, Little Stars has won 12 regional awards for educational excellence.

"Every child who walks through our doors is a star. Our job is simply to help them find their shine. Education is not filling a bucket—it is lighting a fire that lasts a lifetime."
